Computational siRNA Design
Framework

Five phases from target biology to validated candidate — integrating RNA biology context, chemical modification optimization, and closed-loop experimental feedback.

Phase 01
Target Biology & Validation
Upstream Biology
01
Target Gene Identification
YTHDF1/2/3 selected via causal validation — CRISPR knockout in iPSC-derived neurons + eCLIP-seq in postmortem AD brain establishes dominant post-transcriptional regulatory role in AD transcriptome.
CRISPR-KO eCLIP-seq iPSC neurons AD postmortem
02
Cell-Type Specific Expression Context
Neuronal RNA-seq to establish expression levels, isoform landscape, and UTR diversity. Critical because hepatocyte-trained models (GalNAc data) do not generalize to CNS cell types.
RNA-seq Neuronal specificity Isoform resolution
03
Full RNA Biology Characterization of Target
Multi-omics profiling of the target transcript in neuronal context. These datasets serve as guardrails and scoring features — not training labels.
eSHAPE / icSHAPE m6A-eCLIP YTHDF CLIP Ribo-seq AGO2-CLIP
Phase 02
Target Site Selection & Accessibility Scoring
Site Biology
04
RISC Accessibility Scoring Per Site
Structure-weighted accessibility — not just MFE from ViennaRNA, but incorporating experimental SHAPE reactivities and RBP occupancy. Sites occluded by stable hairpins or protein binding are deprioritized regardless of sequence quality.
SHAPE reactivity MFE + accessibility RBP occlusion
05
Multi-Feature Site Ranking
Composite score integrating: structural accessibility, m6A modification context (YTHDF binding affects RISC loading), ribosome occupancy at site, UTR regulatory density, and decay kinetics. This is the core differentiator vs. sequence-only tools.
m6A context Ribo occupancy UTR density Decay kinetics
06
Candidate Site List Generation (~100 sites)
Output: ranked list of ~100 accessible, biologically-contextualized target sites across the transcript. Covers all isoforms. Feeds directly into sequence design phase.
~100 candidates Ranked output Isoform-aware
Phase 03
siRNA Sequence Design & Off-Target Filtering
Sequence Optimization
07
Guide Strand Thermodynamic Optimization
Apply established position rules: end ΔG asymmetry (5′ guide must be less stable than 3′), U at position 1, A/U at positions 10–11 for cleavage efficiency, C at position 19, avoid internal repeats. Seed GC content 30–50%.
End ΔG asymmetry U1 / C19 rules Seed GC 30–50% Duplex stability
08
Off-Target Risk Scoring
Seed-region (positions 2–8) miRNA-like off-target scan across the transcriptome. AGO2-CLIP data from siRNA transfection experiments as empirical ground truth. Penalize guides with seed matches to highly expressed neuronal transcripts. Also score 3′ compensatory binding and partial complementarity.
Seed region scan AGO2-CLIP miReCLIP+ informed 3′ comp. binding
09
Library Reduction: 100 → 20–30 candidates
Combined scoring (site accessibility × thermodynamic quality × off-target penalty) to reduce the library to an experimentally tractable set. This is the primary deliverable of the computational model — replacing empirical brute-force screening.
Composite score 20–30 final candidates Model benchmark output
⚗️
Chemistry Handoff — At this point the sequence is fixed. The problem becomes purely chemical: which modification pattern maximizes nuclease resistance, maintains RISC loading, and survives CNS delivery? This phase is where Discerna / Alnylam-style empirical chemistry applies.
Phase 04
Chemical Modification & Delivery Optimization
Chemistry Layer
10
Modification Pattern Design
For each candidate: optimize 2′-OMe, 2′-F, phosphorothioate backbone placement. The "fluoro pattern" Leonid referenced — empirically derived alternating patterns that maximize nuclease resistance without impairing Ago2 slicer activity. Key gap: ViennaRNA / existing tools were not designed for modified sequences.
2′-OMe 2′-F Phosphorothioate Nuclease resistance
11
Modification-Aware Efficacy Prediction
Predict how each modification pattern affects silencing efficiency. Primary framework gap vs. ENsiRNA and DeepSilencer — neither handles chemically modified sequences natively. Training data: Huesken et al. + OligoGym + any proprietary modified-siRNA activity datasets.
OligoGym Huesken et al. Modified siRNA activity
12
CNS Delivery Compatibility Scoring
Score candidates for BBB penetration potential, LNP encapsulation compatibility, and ICV/intrathecal delivery formats. Reference: Alnylam mivelsiran (AD) CNS delivery pipeline as field benchmark. GalNAc conjugation irrelevant here — CNS requires different chemistry.
BBB penetration LNP / ASO mivelsiran ref. ICV delivery
Phase 05
Experimental Validation & Model Feedback
Closed Loop
13
Experimental Testing in Neuronal Context
IC50 / siEfficacy measurements in iPSC-derived neurons (not HeLa, not hepatocytes). Perturbations: ~100 from the library, measuring knockdown vs. wild-type. siEfficacy score as standard metric for comparison to existing benchmarks.
IC50 siEfficacy iPSC neurons ~100 perturbations
14
Benchmark Comparison — Alpha-Synuclein Reference Case
Use alpha-synuclein (SNCA) as the benchmark transcript. Well-characterized knockdown data exists. Validates the model's site selection and sequence design against known effective siRNAs in neurodegeneration context. Compare predicted rank vs. experimental IC50.
SNCA benchmark Rank correlation Known siRNA data
15
Model Refinement — Closed Loop
Experimental results feed back into the model as labeled training data. On-target efficacy and off-target signal co-optimized across iterations. Each experimental cycle narrows the gap between predicted and actual rank, improving the library reduction quality over time.
Active learning Iterative refinement On-target + off-target
↺
Closed-Loop Feedback Architecture
Experimental outcomes from Phase 5 continuously update the scoring models in Phases 2–4. On-target efficacy improves site selection weights. Off-target outcomes refine the seed-scan penalty. Modified-siRNA activity data improves the chemistry prediction layer. Each cycle: the library reduction becomes more precise, fewer candidates needed.
On-target efficacy → site weights Off-target data → seed penalty Chem activity → mod prediction IC50 → library reduction quality
